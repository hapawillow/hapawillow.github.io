01 Current project

The Notifier

Visit the product ↗ (opens in a new tab)

One platform. Web, mobile, and an alerting API.

A shared mobile app for personal reminders and updates from organizations people choose to follow – without the need for personally identifiable information. The business side brings together an organization dashboard, scheduled alerts, and integrations.

The basic flow for external alerts is:

  1. Customer scans QR code / taps link, which opens the app.
  2. Customer confirms they want alerts from the group.
  3. Backend adds customer to the alert group using an app-generated device ID and push token.
  4. Business schedules an alert from the SaaS.
  5. Cron runs every minute to send scheduled alerts.
  6. A push notification is created and sent to each active member in the alert group.
  7. Alert lands on the customer's phone.
  8. Alert opens the app and shows the alert message, note, and link.

My role Founder & hands-on developer, building with one other engineer.

  • Astro websites and SvelteKit web applications.
  • Expo apps for iOS and Android, including Swift and Kotlin native packages.
  • App and platform security.
  • APIs, alert processes, group and member management, payment processing.
  • Hermes Agent for marketing research and automated outreach emails.
  • A custom WooCommerce plugin that connects stores to The Notifier.

05TuneGO DashboardInternal business intelligenceSvelteKit / Turso / ApexCharts

Make business health easier to read.

Internal dashboards for business performance, revenue, and KPIs, refreshed every ten minutes. It was built to replace the existing AWS QuickSight dashboard and showcase low-cost / free tools to reduce company expenses.

It cost $0/mo and ran on Vercel. The dashboards did not have direct access to the company’s production systems. A cron ran a Python script to extract and summarize data from the AWS RDS read-replica to the dashboard's Turso database. It used a simple authorization and access control system: OTP, restricted to company email addresses, and whitelists.

Technical focus: SvelteKit interfaces, ApexCharts visualizations, Drizzle data access, and passwordless sign-in with Lucia and Loops.
